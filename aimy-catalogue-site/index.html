The Support Reality Paradigm

Compare the chaotic reality of manual support floors against the structure of an AiMY-driven ecosystem.

Day 0 Baseline Support Floor

  • Scattered Knowledge Silos: Shared folders, senior agents' heads, and Slack threads with no unified source of truth.
  • Invisible Voice Channels: Live calls happen inside a black box with zero quality oversight or coaching loop.
  • Selective Sample-Based QA: Reviewing only 1-2% of weekly interactions, catching critical errors days after they occurred.
  • Reactive Management Firefighting: Supervisors struggle to track floor metrics and only step in during severe escalations.

Day 30 AiMY Support Floor

  • Factual Knowledge Foundation: A unified knowledge layer indexing FAQs and SOPs securely for instant, consistent answers.
  • Intelligent Conversational Voice: Call recording, native live transcriptions, and conversational coaching models.
  • 100% Automated QA Auditing: Automated evaluating and scoring across every email, chat, and phone interaction.
  • Proactive Operations Control: Real-time status trackers, predictive dashboards, and immediate SLA alerts.
